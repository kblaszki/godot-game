# Survival crafting (lab)

## Sim

- Gather nodes, inventory, recipes, needs meters on FixedTick.
- World gen seed (`procedural-gen`); base/building as data grid.
- Persist world seed + inventories (`save-systems`).

## App

- World view 2D/3D; craft UI (`game-ui-ux`); item icons (`game-assets`).

## Compose

`procedural-gen` · `sim-data-resources` · `save-systems` · `level-design` · `game-ui-ux` · `godot-2d-presentation` or `godot-3d-presentation`
